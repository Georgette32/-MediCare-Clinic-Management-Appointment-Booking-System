# Business rules

- A booking requires configured doctor working hours, and cannot overlap an active appointment, break, or approved leave. SQL Server serializable transaction is used for the availability check and insert.
- Default consultation duration is 20 minutes until at least five valid completed consultation samples exist. The latest 30 are used; sample variance and standard deviation are calculated. With enough data, slot duration is `ceil(mean + 0.5 × standard deviation)` by default. The multiplier is per-doctor configurable. Long consultations are retained in the sample.
- Grace Period is `ceil(consultation duration × doctor GracePeriodMultiplier)` from scheduled start; multiplier defaults to 2. Arrival at the boundary remains eligible. After the boundary, the appointment keeps its audit record but its queue attendance is converted to a walk-in candidate requiring explicit doctor approval.
- First clinic call is permitted before any consultation has started that day. Subsequent calls require an active consultation and only one unexpired call may exist at a time. Call response expires after five minutes. A hosted worker processes timeouts; it does not mark NoShow while Grace Period remains active.
- An active consultation cannot be interrupted. When choosing queue order, eligible booked patients precede approved walk-ins. Booked ordering is scheduled time then check-in; walk-ins are FIFO by arrival.
- Waiting room has three seats by default, excluding the patient in consultation. Capacity is checked inside a serializable transaction. Walk-ins cannot consume the configured reserved booked capacity.
- Cancellation recovery chooses the oldest active matching waiting-list registration. The provisional offer lifetime is 15 minutes. An accepted claim verifies slot availability and creates the appointment in the same transaction. Offer notifications appear in the local patient inbox; connect this adapter to the team Notifications service before shared database integration.
- Persisted timestamps are UTC. Scheduling inputs and working-hour comparisons currently treat clock values as UTC because no clinic time-zone contract existed in the empty repository.
